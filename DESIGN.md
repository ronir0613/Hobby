# Visual foundation

The app uses a custom, quiet visual system: warm paper surfaces, botanical green accents, generous space, rounded cards, and Hanken Grotesk. There are no progress streaks, productivity scores, gradients, or social counters.

## Shared tokens

`src/theme/tokens.json` is the source of truth for light and dark colors. `src/theme/colors.ts` exposes the typed palettes to React Native; `tailwind.config.js` maps the same values to NativeWind utility classes. Light text and controls use a tested high-contrast palette, with a system dark-mode counterpart.

## Interface decisions

- The four primary tabs are Discover, Explore, Media, and Profile.
- Explore reads the catalogue (10 categories, 68 interests, their starter ideas) from Supabase, with a loading line, a muted error box with "Try again", and an empty state. Search and category chips filter the loaded catalogue. Discover reads the same catalogue (same loading and error states): a featured "A gentle place to start" card and two "Or start anywhere" cards, each from a different category, rotating one step a day at local midnight until the quiz ranks them (step 15). The catalogue is public, so all of this works signed out.
- Tapping an interest card opens its detail screen: category, name, description, then quiet fact pills (muted, 16/8 padding: hobby/habit, Indoors / Outdoors / Indoors or out, On your own / With others / Alone or together, "About 30 min", Free / Low cost / Some kit), "What you'll need" (17pt semibold heading, one secondary line), and "Ways to begin": the starter ideas easiest first as cards (difficulty "Easy" / "A bit more" / "A stretch" with the time, the title, and numbered steps). Only the first idea is open; the others fold to their difficulty, time and title with a chevron and open on tap (48dp button, `aria-expanded`), so the screen reads as one next step. Sections sit 32 apart, cards 12 apart. Then "Try this" (it links the attempt to the first idea). "Try this" adds a planned attempt; there is one per interest, so if it is already in My hobbies the button reads "Open in My hobbies", and a completed or dropped one offers "Give it another go" (back to planned, history kept, a new go starts with the next check-in). Signed out it works the same, saved on the phone (the hint under the button says so). Success uses the soft green notice box.
- Profile starts with "My hobbies" (from the account, or from the phone when signed out), split into sections by status (Active, Planned, Completed, Dropped, in that order, each with a 17pt semibold heading; empty sections are hidden): a card per attempt with the interest name, a status pill (Active: accent-soft with accent text; Planned and Dropped: muted; Completed: soft green) and the latest check-in. A card opens the attempt screen: "How did today go?" is a Tried / Skipped segmented control for today only (switchable until midnight); "Your days" lists past answers read-only; "Where is it at?" is a read-only list of the four statuses with how each is reached, the current one marked. Status follows the answers: 7 tried days completes it ("You gave this a real go."), two skipped days in a row drops it ("Maybe it wasn't your thing. That's okay." with "Give it another go" and "Find something new").
- Reminders is its own screen, opened from a "Gentle reminders" row on Profile (below My hobbies) that sums them up ("Evenings at 8:00 pm · Weekdays", or "Off"). It has a card with a Morning nudge and an Evening check-in, each with a switch (accent track, light thumb) and a time row that opens the Android clock dialog, then "On these days": seven round 48dp day chips, Monday first, chosen ones filled with the accent; the last chosen day can't be turned off. Changes save straight away. A denied notification permission or a failed save uses the muted error box (with "Open phone settings" for permission). Signed out, the settings are kept on the phone and scheduled the same way.
- Notes and photos belong to an attempt. Its screen has a "Notes and photos" section (below today's answer): a multi-line "New note" field with "Save note", each note as a card with its day, the text, and Edit (the card becomes the field with Save / Cancel) and Delete; then "Take a photo" and "Choose from gallery" (Android photo picker, no storage permission; the camera asks once, and a denied camera uses the muted box with "Open phone settings"), and a 3-column grid of square thumbnails. A thumbnail opens the photo full screen with "Delete photo". Deleting a note or photo asks first with the system dialog ("Delete this photo?" / "It will be removed for good."); the trigger is a secondary button, since there is no danger colour. Photos are downscaled to 2048 px JPEG before upload.
- Media lists every hobby as a card (name, "2 notes · 1 photo" or "Nothing kept yet", up to three thumbnails and the latest note's first line); the card opens the attempt screen, where notes and photos are added. With no hobbies it shows the keepsakes empty state and "Find an idea to try". Nothing needs an account: signed out (guest mode), hobbies, check-ins, notes, photos and reminder settings are kept on the phone, and the attempt screen's notes say "Kept privately on this phone." Profile shows My hobbies and Reminders either way; below them, the signed-in account (sign out, delete account with confirmation), or for a guest a "Back up and sync" card with "Sign in" and a quiet "Clear this phone's data" (confirmed with the system dialog). Right after sign-in, Profile shows "Moving what you saved on this phone to your account…" while the guest data uploads, and the muted box with "Try again" if it stops (the data stays on the phone).
- The tab bar pairs text labels with `expo-symbols` icons (Material Symbols on Android: spa, search, photo_library, person). The active tab is marked by the accent colour. Buttons, chips, and filter controls have a 48dp minimum target height.
- Sign-in is a full screen opened from Profile: Google first (Android's native account sheet, no browser), then email and password with a sign-in / create-account switch. Errors use plain, non-blaming copy in a muted box; there is no separate danger colour yet.
- System font scaling remains enabled, page titles expose heading roles, and input and category controls have accessibility labels and states.
- The search field boundary keeps at least 3:1 contrast against its surface, and the active tab label at least 4.5:1, in both themes; those ratios are covered by a small token test.
