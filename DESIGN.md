# Visual foundation

The app uses a custom, quiet visual system: warm paper surfaces, botanical green accents, generous space, few boxes, a flat drawing for each hobby, and Hanken Grotesk. Screens open with a plain title (no uppercase eyebrows or taglines). There are no progress streaks, productivity scores, gradients, or social counters.

## Shared tokens

`src/theme/tokens.json` is the source of truth for light and dark colors, the type scale, spacing and radii. `src/theme/colors.ts` exposes the typed palettes and `typeScale` / `space` / `radius` to React Native; `tailwind.config.js` maps the colors and radii to NativeWind classes (`rounded-card` 24, `rounded-tile` 16). Light text and controls use a tested high-contrast palette, with a system dark-mode counterpart.

- Type: `AppText` takes a `variant`: display 32/40 (page titles), title 24/32 (featured card), heading 20/28 (sections), body 16/24, small 14/20 (details, captions), label 12/16 (tab bar). About three sizes per screen; weight is regular, plus semibold for titles and emphasis.
- Space: 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64. Page gutter 24, card padding 24, compact tiles 16, sections 32 apart (48 on Discover and Profile), rows in a group separated by hairlines instead of a box each (`ListGroup` / `ListRow`).
- Illustrations: `HobbyArt` draws one flat SVG per hobby (100, in `src/components/art/hobby-art.ts`): one large object you can recognise, on a stronger colour field set per category (15, each with a light and a dark value; the objects keep their colours in both themes). They are decorative and hidden from screen readers. A hobby no longer listed gets a plain leaf. A test checks that every catalogue slug has its own drawing.
- Motion: `PressableScale` is the shared press feedback (scale to 98% and dim 12% while held, no animation; with reduce motion only the dim). `Pressable` must not get a `style` function: NativeWind on Android drops it when `className` is present, so inline styles and press feedback vanish. Use `PressableScale` (a test guards this). Discover's picks fade in over 240 ms; screen transitions stay the native defaults.
- Brand: the "hobby" script logo (`assets/brand`) is the app icon, the adaptive icon (cream on green), the monochrome/notification icon, the splash (green on paper, cream on dark) and the favicon.

## Interface decisions

- The four primary tabs are Discover, Explore, Media, and Profile.
- Explore (title "Explore") reads the catalogue (15 categories, 100 hobbies, their starter ideas) from Supabase, with a loading line, a muted error box with "Try again", and an empty state. Search and category chips filter the loaded catalogue; each interest is a card with its drawing, name, category and a two-line description. Discover reads the same catalogue (same loading and error states): a featured card (illustration, name, description, "First step:" and the first idea) and two smaller cards (illustration, name, "5 min to begin"), each from a different category, then a quiet "See all 100 interests" row, then the locked "100 days, 100 hobbies" card (lock icon, "One new hobby a day. Coming later."; muted, with no action yet). With quiz answers the title is "Picked for you" with the featured pick's reason under it ("Calm, indoors, 15 minutes or less.", up to three matching tags; ties rotate daily) and the small cards sit under "More for you". Without answers it is "A place to start" with a quiet underlined "Get picks that suit you", rotating one step a day at local midnight, and the small cards sit under "Or start anywhere". The catalogue is public, so all of this works signed out.
- The starting quiz opens once on this phone's first launch, over Discover: five questions, one per screen (what it should feel like; indoors or out; on your own or with others; how much time; how much to spend), each a 3–4 option list of 56dp cards (radio role; the chosen one fills with the accent and a check). A tap advances after a short beat, the last tap saves and closes. "Question 2 of 5" with a thin accent bar sits above the 32pt question; a back arrow (and Android back) returns to the previous question with its answer kept. "Skip" (top right, underlined) closes it and keeps the rotation; skipping, closing or finishing all count as seen, so it doesn't return. Profile has a "Your picks" row ("Retake the quiz" or "Take the quiz", with a summary like "Calm · Indoors · On my own · 15 min"); a retake starts with the earlier answers chosen and "Cancel" in place of Skip. A failed save (signed in, offline) shows the muted box with "Try again".
- Tapping an interest card opens its detail screen: its whole drawing (the frame keeps the drawing's 16:10 shape, rounded), the name as the page title, "Arts and crafts · a hobby" under it, the description, then four facts with icons in two columns (Indoors / Outdoors / Indoors or out, On your own / With others / Alone or together, "About 30 min", Free / Low cost / Some kit), "What you'll need" (heading, one secondary line, then a muted box with a warning icon for the 15 hobbies with a safety note), and "Ways to begin": the starter ideas easiest first as rows of one surface, each with its title and "Easy · About 2 min" ("A bit more", "A stretch"). Every step is a direct instruction that says exactly what to do. Under the steps, any reference links show their title (underlined accent) and publisher with an open-in-new icon (48dp row); a link opens in the phone's browser with `Linking.openURL`, and if that fails a plain line under it says so. Only 92 of the 300 ideas have a link, since unverified or weak guides were left out. The first is open with numbered steps; the others fold with a chevron and open on tap (48dp button, `aria-expanded`), so the screen reads as one next step. Then "Try this" (it links the attempt to the first idea). "Try this" adds a planned attempt; there is one per interest, so if it is already in My hobbies the button reads "Open in My hobbies", and a completed or dropped one offers "Give it another go" (back to planned, history kept, a new go starts with the next check-in). Signed out it works the same, saved on the phone (the hint under the button says so). Success uses the soft green notice box.
- Profile (title "Profile") starts with "My hobbies" (from the account, or from the phone when signed out): one surface with a row per attempt, sorted by status (Active, Planned, Completed, Dropped), each with the hobby's thumbnail, the interest name, the latest check-in and a status pill (Active: accent-soft with accent text; Planned and Dropped: muted with a hairline border; Completed: soft green). With none it is "Nothing here yet." and "Browse interests". Then "Preferences" (Gentle reminders and the quiz row on one surface), then the account or "Back up and sync". A card opens the attempt screen: "How did today go?" is a Tried / Skipped segmented control for today only (switchable until midnight); "Your days" lists past answers read-only; "Where is it at?" is a read-only list of the four statuses with how each is reached, the current one marked. Status follows the answers: 7 tried days completes it ("You gave this a real go."), two skipped days in a row drops it ("Maybe it wasn't your thing. That's okay." with "Give it another go" and "Find something new").
- Reminders is its own screen, opened from a "Gentle reminders" row on Profile (below My hobbies) that sums them up ("Evenings at 8:00 pm · Weekdays", or "Off"). It has a card with a Morning nudge and an Evening check-in, each with a switch (accent track, light thumb) and a time row (the time and an underlined "Change", no inner box) that opens the Android clock dialog, then the "On these days" heading: seven round 48dp day chips, Monday first, chosen ones filled with the accent; the last chosen day can't be turned off. Changes save straight away. A denied notification permission or a failed save uses the muted error box (with "Open phone settings" for permission). Signed out, the settings are kept on the phone and scheduled the same way.
- Notes and photos belong to an attempt. Its screen has a "Notes and photos" section (below today's answer): a multi-line "New note" field with "Save note", each note as a card with its day, the text, and Edit (the card becomes the field with Save / Cancel) and Delete; then "Take a photo" and "Choose from gallery" (Android photo picker, no storage permission; the camera asks once, and a denied camera uses the muted box with "Open phone settings"), and a 3-column grid of square thumbnails. A thumbnail opens the photo full screen with "Delete photo". Deleting a note or photo asks first with the system dialog ("Delete this photo?" / "It will be removed for good."); the trigger is a secondary button, since there is no danger colour. Photos are downscaled to 2048 px JPEG before upload.
- Media (title "Media", "Notes and photos from your hobbies.") lists every hobby as a card (the hobby's thumbnail, name, chevron, "2 notes · 1 photo" or "Nothing kept yet", up to three thumbnails and the latest note's first line); the card opens the attempt screen, where notes and photos are added. With no hobbies it shows one line ("Your keepsakes will go here.") and "Find an idea to try". Nothing needs an account: signed out (guest mode), hobbies, check-ins, notes, photos and reminder settings are kept on the phone, and the attempt screen's notes say "Kept privately on this phone." Profile shows My hobbies and Reminders either way; below them, the signed-in account (sign out, delete account with confirmation), or for a guest a "Back up your hobbies" card ("Saved on this phone only. Sign in to keep them safe.") with "Sign in" and a quiet "Clear this phone's data" (confirmed with the system dialog). Right after sign-in, Profile shows "Moving what you saved on this phone to your account…" while the guest data uploads, and the muted box with "Try again" if it stops (the data stays on the phone).
- The tab bar pairs text labels with `expo-symbols` icons (Material Symbols on Android: spa, search, photo_library, person). The active tab is marked by the accent colour. Buttons, chips, and filter controls have a 48dp minimum target height (`min-h-12` is set to 48px in `tailwind.config.js`, since NativeWind counts 1rem as 14 on native).
- Sign-in is a full screen opened from Profile: Google first (Android's native account sheet, no browser), then email and password with a sign-in / create-account switch. Errors use plain, non-blaming copy in a muted box; there is no separate danger colour yet.
- System font scaling remains enabled, page titles expose heading roles, and input and category controls have accessibility labels and states.
- The search field boundary keeps at least 3:1 contrast against its surface, and the active tab label at least 4.5:1, in both themes; those ratios are covered by a small token test.
