# Hobby privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Hobby is an Android app that helps you find a hobby and try it. It is made by ZRonin. This page explains what the app keeps, where it goes and how to remove it.

## The short version

- You can use Hobby without an account. What you save then stays on your phone.
- If you sign in, what you save is stored on our server so you can back it up and sync it.
- There are no ads and no analytics. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
- You can delete your account and everything saved to it from inside the app.

## If you don't sign in

Your hobbies, check-ins, notes, photos, reminder settings and quiz answers are kept on your phone. We don't receive them.

Android's own device backup doesn't include them. If you uninstall the app or change phones without signing in, they're gone.

## If you sign in

When you first sign in, what you saved on your phone moves to your account. We then store:

- **Your account details.** Your email address and a user ID. With email sign-in, also your password, which is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form. With Google sign-in, also your name and the link to your Google profile picture, which Google sends when you sign in. The app doesn't show them.
- **Your hobby activity.** The hobbies you try, your daily "tried" or "skipped" answers, and your quiz answers.
- **What you write and add.** Your notes and photos. Photos are private to your account.
- **Your reminder settings.** Reminder times, chosen days and time zone. The reminders themselves are scheduled on your phone.

We use this only to run the app: to sign you in, keep your data safe and show it on your phone.

## Permissions

- **Camera**, only if you choose "Take a photo".
- **Notifications**, only if you turn on reminders.
- **Photos.** The app uses Android's photo picker, so it only sees the photos you pick. On Android 9 and older, the camera feature also asks for storage access, because of how the camera tool works on those versions.

## Services we use

We don't sell or share your data, but three companies handle some of it to make the app work:

- **Supabase** hosts accounts, data and photos. Our project is in Singapore, so your data may be stored outside your country.
- **Google** handles "Sign in with Google", if you choose it.
- **Expo** delivers app updates. To check for updates, the app sends Expo a random ID created at install. It also sends basic details such as its version and platform. This doesn't include your account or anything you've saved.

Like most online services, these companies may keep short-term technical logs that include your internet (IP) address. Data travels to them over encrypted connections.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete it. Once you delete your account, it's removed straight away from the live service. It may stay in the service's backups for a limited time until they expire.

## How to delete your account and data

**In the app**

1. Open the Profile tab.
2. Scroll to "Delete account" and tap "Delete my account".
3. Confirm with "Delete account".

This permanently removes your account, hobbies, check-ins, notes, photos, reminder settings and quiz answers. It can't be undone.

**Without the app**

Email curatedclass13@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm when it's done, within 30 days.

**If you never signed in**

Open the Profile tab and tap "Clear this phone's data", or uninstall the app. We hold nothing to delete.

## Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your data or ask us to correct it by emailing curatedclass13@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law.

## Children

Hobby isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
